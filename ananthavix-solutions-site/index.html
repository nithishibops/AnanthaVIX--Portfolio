About

Where operations knowledge meets analytical build.

We work across banking alliances, merchant onboarding, payment gateway operations, and KYC/AML compliance — the parts of a financial business that don't show up in a pitch deck, but decide whether it actually runs day to day.

Ananthavix Solutions is where that operational depth turns into something usable: Power BI dashboards that replace manual MIS, VBA automation that removes recurring reporting effort, AML/KYC review frameworks, and full internal platforms — like the HRMS system below — designed and shipped end to end.

Between us, that spans investor operations and Corporate Actions processing at an RTA, AML/KYC and data-consolidation work on an HDFC Mutual Fund account, and active banking-alliance and merchant-onboarding roles today.
