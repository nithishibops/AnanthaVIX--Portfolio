Privacy & data handling

This notice describes the current public Ananthavix Solutions website. It should be updated when client accounts, analytics, forms, cookies or additional data-processing services are introduced.

Website enquiries

The website enquiry widget prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. Email and telephone links open the relevant service on your device. Information you choose to send through WhatsApp, email or telephone is handled through those third-party services and used to respond to your enquiry.

Public website data

The current static website does not provide a user account, client database or public file-upload function. We do not intentionally publish client business data, credentials, private source code or confidential implementation files.

Client projects

For paid or private client work, data access, storage, hosting, retention and confidentiality should be defined in the applicable proposal, agreement or project documentation. Client business data remains separate from public portfolio/demo data.

Third-party services

The site may link to services such as WhatsApp, email, Google Play or externally hosted demonstrations. Those services have their own privacy terms and practices.

Updates
