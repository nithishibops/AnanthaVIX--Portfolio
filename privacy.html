Privacy & data handling

How website enquiries and project information are handled.

Website enquiries

The website enquiry form prepares an email draft in your email application. Information is not submitted to a server by this form. Review the draft before sending. Information you choose to send by email is handled through your email provider and used to respond to your enquiry.

Public website data

The current static website does not provide a user account, client database or public file-upload function. We do not intentionally publish client business data, credentials, private source code or confidential implementation files.

Client projects

For paid or private client work, data access, storage, hosting, retention and confidentiality should be defined in the applicable proposal, agreement or project documentation. Client business data remains separate from public portfolio/demo data.

Third-party services

This website may link to email services, Google Play or externally hosted demonstrations, and loads fonts from Google Fonts when available. Those services have their own privacy terms and practices.

Updates

This notice may be updated as the website and business systems evolve. For privacy questions, contact nithish.ibops@gmail.com.
